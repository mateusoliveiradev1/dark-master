# Hooks — Mistérios inexplicados

> Sistema: `references/31` (Shorts) + `03` (Y1–Y11). Use o arquétipo, não a frase.
> Exemplos em EN — adapte ao caso com fatos verificados antes de usar. Tom: curiosidade + ceticismo. Nunca afirmar causa sobrenatural ou conspiração; relato nunca vira fato no hook.

## Long-form (10 ganchos)

Cada gancho: 1–2 frases, sem data/local antes do gancho, sem abstração.

1. "The boat was found drifting with the engine still running. The three men who left the dock were never seen again."
2. "Two radars tracked it for six minutes. The report that survived lists an empty sky."
3. "The recording lasts forty-seven seconds. Halfway through, every instrument in the room reads the same impossible number."
4. "The logbook has an entry every morning for eleven days. The twelfth stops in the middle of a sentence."
5. "Twelve people watched the same light cross the same field. Two of them drew it that night, before talking to anyone."
6. "The photograph is real, and so is the timestamp. The problem is where it puts him."
7. "She described the house street by street, years before she could read. The house had been empty since the fifties."
8. "Seventy people reported the same sound at the same minute. The nearest machine that could make it sat fifty miles away."
9. "Three separate labs measured the same anomaly across three decades. Their papers agree on what happened and disagree on why."
10. "The search party found the camp untouched, the fire still warm, and one page missing from the journal."

## Shorts — frame 1 (15 hooks)

| # | Arquétipo (ref 31) | Texto na tela (≤6) | Falado (≤8) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Impossibilidade completa | ENGINE STILL RUNNING | "The engine was running. The crew was gone." |
| 2 | Pergunta em 2ª pessoa | WOULD YOU SEARCH? | "Would you search a place that gives nothing back?" |
| 3 | Contradição verificada | CLEAR SKY. TWO RADARS. | "Two radars saw it. Report says clear sky." |
| 4 | Número + stake | NINE DAYS. NO TRACE. | "Nine days searching. Nothing but footprints." |
| 5 | Relógio/countdown | 47 SECONDS | "Forty-seven seconds. Every gauge reads the same." |
| 6 | Antes/depois impossível | STAMPED 3 AM | "Photo stamped 3 AM. He was asleep on camera." |
| 7 | Objeto-símbolo | ONE GLOVE | "One glove found. Its pair, forty miles away." |
| 8 | Citação/diálogo | "WE HEARD SINGING" | "'We heard singing,' the log says. Then nothing." |
| 9 | POV/imersão | YOU'RE THE WITNESS | "You're the only one awake when it starts." |
| 10 | Segredo/proibido | SEALED 60 YEARS | "The file was sealed for sixty years." |
| 11 | Loop declarado | WATCH THE CLOCK | "Watch the clock. It skips one minute." |
| 12 | Erro sistêmico | WRONG NIGHT | "They searched the wrong night for forty years." |
| 13 | Micro-história (3 frases) | 9:00 → NEVER CAME | "He left at nine. He never came home." |
| 14 | Escolha impossível | SEARCH AGAIN? | "Search again, or wait one more night." |
| 15 | Objeto-símbolo | THE LAST ENTRY | "The log stops mid-sentence." |

## Loops (5 emendas)

1. **Objeto + som:** último frame repete o objeto do frame 1 (livro de registro, luva) na mesma posição; a última palavra ("gone") ecoa a primeira.
2. **Pergunta eco:** o frame 1 abre "What happened to them?" e o payoff fecha com a mesma pergunta respondida pela metade — o resto só no long.
3. **Número espelho:** o número do frame 1 (nove dias, quarenta e sete segundos) volta como última fala antes do corte ("nine days, to the hour").
4. **Cena espelho:** a janela/sala do frame 1 reaparece no último frame com a mesma luz e o mesmo enquadramento (emenda visual).
5. **Loop aberto:** terminar no meio da frase do arquivo ("…and that's when the second signal appeared") — o início mostra a chegada do primeiro.

## CTAs (que não quebram o loop)

- **Falado (fim):** "The document I didn't quote — full file tonight, on the channel."
- **Comentário fixado:** pergunta do caso + link do long ("Which explanation survives the timeline? Full file: [link]").
- **Related Video:** apontar para o long do dia (nunca para o canal em geral).

## Proibido neste nicho

- Afirmar conspiração, causa sobrenatural ou fraude como fato — em qualquer camada (fala, texto, thumbnail, descrição).
- Footage falso "caught on camera", recriação apresentada como registro real, IA sem rótulo quando for recriação.
- Gore, corpo, close de ferimento; exploração de vítimas e famílias; nome de pessoa viva ligada a caso sem "alleged"/"claimed".
- "You won't believe", "shocking", "they don't want you to know" e similares; ALL CAPS para forjar intensidade.
- Data/local antes do gancho; "in this video"; CTA genérico ("subscribe now"); prometer o que o episódio não entrega.
