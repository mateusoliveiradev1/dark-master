# Hooks — Psicologia dark (experimentos, comportamento e manipulação)

> Sistema: `references/31` (Shorts) + `03` (Y1–Y11). Use o arquétipo, não a frase.
> Regra do nicho: todo fato do hook precisa existir na pesquisa do episódio com fonte. Hook sem entrega derruba a série inteira.

## Long-form (10 ganchos)

Cada gancho: 1–2 frases, sem data/local antes do gancho, sem abstração.

1. Sixty-five percent of ordinary people flipped the final switch. Four decades later, researchers went back to the tapes and found a different story.
2. A teacher, a whistle and five days. That is how a normal high school became a movement no one could stop.
3. Ten strangers on a raft and a scientist who wrote that he hoped they would turn on each other. By week two, they were best friends.
4. Identical quadruplets, one diagnosis, one famous study — and a question the researchers never answered.
5. Twelve children. Six diagnoses. One genome that researchers still study today.
6. A researcher taught a baby to fear a white rat. The review that would have stopped the study did not exist yet.
7. One student started laughing. Within weeks, hundreds were laughing with her and the school had to close. There was no virus.
8. Two summer camps, one trophy, and scientists who engineered a war between boys who had never met.
9. Most of what you know about the Stanford prison experiment comes from one researcher's account. The archival tapes complicate the story.
10. Eight people sealed inside a glass dome for two years. The oxygen dropped, the food ran low, and the crew split in two.

## Loops (5 emendas)

1. **Visual + sonora:** o último quadro repete a composição do primeiro (a mesa do laboratório) e a palavra final emenda a primeira fala.
2. **Loop aberto:** termina em "So what would you have done?" e o topo abre com "There is one question this file keeps asking."
3. **Emenda sonora:** a primeira palavra ("Obedience.") volta como última ("...and they still call it obedience.").
4. **Visual:** corte no close do objeto-símbolo idêntico ao frame 1 (botão, máscara, porta).
5. **Contagem:** o fim fecha em "Day one." e o começo abre em "Day one." de novo.

## CTAs (que não quebram o loop)

- **Falado (fim):** "The document I didn't read — pinned comment, full source file."
- **Comentário fixado:** pergunta do caso ("Would you have flipped the switch? Be honest.") + link do long.
- **Related Video:** aponta para o long do dia.

## Proibido neste nicho

- Tutorial de manipulação ou "dark psychology tricks".
- Diagnóstico do espectador ou de qualquer pessoa real.
- Gore, descrição gráfica de sofrimento, autolesão ou suicídio como foco.
- Nome ou imagem de pessoa viva fora de contexto público documentado; detalhe clínico privado.
- CTA genérico ("inscreva-se", "deixe o like") e promessa que o frame 1 não mostra.
