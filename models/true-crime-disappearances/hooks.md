# Hooks — Desaparecimentos

> Sistema: `references/31` (Shorts) + `03` (Y1–Y11). Use o arquétipo, não a frase. Exemplos em EN (idioma do canal).

## Long-form (10 ganchos)

Cada gancho: 1–2 frases, sem data/local antes do gancho, sem abstração.

1. The car was still running. Her purse was on the passenger seat. She was gone.
2. He left a voicemail saying he could see the town lights. Search teams never found the road he described.
3. A locked apartment, a phone left charging, and a debit card used 400 miles away the next morning.
4. The search dogs stopped at the same fence line three times. Nobody could explain why.
5. She called 911 and said someone was following her. The call ended before she gave an address.
6. One shoe. That's what the searchers logged — half a mile from where she was last seen.
7. The file stayed sealed for twenty years. When it opened, it answered one question and created five more.
8. He was on camera at 2:03 a.m. walking toward the parking garage. The garage camera never recorded him leaving.
9. Investigators spent six years searching the wrong county. The correction came from a retired bus driver.
10. She boarded the ship with her family and a suitcase for a seven-day cruise. Only the suitcase came back.

## Shorts — frame 1 (15 hooks)

| # | Arquétipo (ref 31) | Texto na tela (≤6) | Falado (≤8) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Impossibilidade completa | LOCKED FROM INSIDE | Her keys were inside. The door was locked from inside. |
| 2 | Pergunta em 2ª pessoa | WOULD YOU STOP? | Would you pull over for a stranger at 2 a.m.? |
| 3 | Contradição verificada | PHONE STILL ON | Her phone kept charging. She never answered again. |
| 4 | Número + stake | THREE. ONE. ZERO. | Three women. One road. Zero answers. |
| 5 | Relógio/countdown | 9:04 → NEVER | He left at nine. By 9:04, he was gone. |
| 6 | Antes/depois impossível | 2 HOURS LATER | This photo was taken two hours after she vanished. |
| 7 | Objeto-símbolo | ONE SHOE | All they ever found was one shoe. |
| 8 | Citação/diálogo | CALL NEVER CAME | "Call you back," he said. The call never came. |
| 9 | POV/imersão | LAST TO SEE HER | You're the last person who saw her leave. |
| 10 | Segredo/proibido | SEALED 20 YEARS | This case file stayed sealed for twenty years. |
| 11 | Loop declarado | WATCH THE TIMESTAMP | Look at the timestamp. It's still 2:03. |
| 12 | Erro sistêmico | WRONG COUNTY | They searched the wrong county for six years. |
| 13 | Micro-história (3 frases) | 9:00 → 9:04 | He left at nine. She called at 9:04. |
| 14 | Escolha impossível | SAVE ONE | Report the sighting or protect the witness. |
| 15 | Objeto-símbolo (variação) | ONE CALL | One phone call could have changed everything. |

## Loops (5 emendas)

1. **Visual + sonora:** último frame é o mesmo plano do primeiro (corredor/porta vazia) e a última palavra repete a primeira ("gone" → "gone").
2. **Loop aberto:** terminar no meio da frase — "…and the second call came from her own number" — o início diz "the first call".
3. **Emenda de beat:** o impacto sonoro do fim cai exatamente no tempo da primeira palavra do hook; a frase fecha onde começou.
4. **Emenda de objeto:** o objeto-símbolo (chave, sapato, crachá) reaparece no último frame na mesma posição do frame 1.
5. **Emenda de pergunta:** o Short termina repetindo a pergunta do frame 1, em outro tom ("Who called at 9:04?").

## CTAs (que não quebram o loop)

- **Falado (fim):** "The four minutes I left out — tonight, full timeline." (específico do caso, como em `31`)
- **Comentário fixado:** pergunta do caso + link do long: "Where does the timeline break for you? Full case file → [link]"
- **Related Video:** apontar para o long do dia (nunca CTA genérico).
- **Nunca:** "inscreva-se agora", "deixe o like", CTA de canal.

## Proibido neste nicho

- Gore ou imagem de cena de crime no hook e na thumb (yellow icon).
- Nome de pessoa viva sem "alleged/suspect" (difamação).
- Prometer resolução ("I solved it") ou vender teoria como fato.
- Contato com familiares sem consentimento; expor menores.
- Repetir as mesmas palavras do long no hook do Short (o espectador percebe o corte).
