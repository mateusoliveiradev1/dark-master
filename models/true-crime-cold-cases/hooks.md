# Hooks — Cold cases (EUA)

> Sistema: `references/31` (Shorts) + `03` (Y1–Y11). Use o arquétipo, não a frase.
> Exemplos de arquétipo em EN — adapte ao caso com fatos verificados antes de usar. Sem gore, sem sensacionalismo.

## Long-form (10 ganchos)

Cada gancho: 1–2 frases, sem data/local antes do gancho, sem abstração.

1. "All the lab had left was four nanograms of DNA, and one company willing to try anyway."
2. "Twenty-three years passed between the bite mark and the arrest. The swab never left the building."
3. "She was found on a rainy street. Parts of her coat were bone dry."
4. "One stocking cap waited fifty years to name a killer."
5. "'That kind of evidence doesn't get thrown away,' the criminalist said. Then the file went quiet for nineteen years."
6. "She left for the bus stop at six in the morning. Four days later, the search ended forty miles away."
7. "In 1983, the evidence was unreadable. The technology to read it hadn't been invented yet."
8. "How long would you keep calling a department that stopped answering?"
9. "The case went cold in a room full of evidence nobody could read."
10. "Fifty years later, the answer came from a family tree, not a fingerprint."

## Shorts — frame 1 (15 hooks)

| # | Arquétipo (ref 31) | Texto na tela (≤6) | Falado (≤8) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Impossibilidade completa | LOCKED FROM INSIDE | "Locked from the inside. No one inside." |
| 2 | Pergunta em 2ª pessoa | WOULD YOU REOPEN IT? | "Would you reopen a case closed in 1974?" |
| 3 | Contradição verificada | COAT WAS DRY | "Rain all night. Her coat was dry." |
| 4 | Número + stake | FOUR NANOGRAMS | "Four nanograms left. The lab needed more." |
| 5 | Relógio/countdown | 23 YEARS | "Twenty-three years. The swab never left the freezer." |
| 6 | Antes/depois impossível | 50 YEARS LATER | "The name arrived fifty years after the funeral." |
| 7 | Objeto-símbolo | ONE STOCKING CAP | "One stocking cap held the only clue." |
| 8 | Citação/diálogo | NEVER THROW THOSE OUT | "'We never throw those out,' he said." |
| 9 | POV/imersão | LAST CALL | "You're the last call she ever made." |
| 10 | Segredo/proibido | SEALED SINCE 1986 | "This file was sealed for decades." |
| 11 | Loop declarado | WATCH THE TAG | "Watch the evidence tag. It shows up again." |
| 12 | Erro sistêmico | WRONG DATABASE | "They compared his DNA to the wrong database." |
| 13 | Micro-história (3 frases) | 6:00 → NO BUS | "She left at six. She never got on." |
| 14 | Escolha impossível | NAME OR SILENCE | "Tell the family now, or wait for certainty." |
| 15 | Objeto-símbolo | ONE SWAB | "One swab stayed sealed for nineteen years." |

## Loops (5 emendas)

1. **Objeto + som:** último frame repete o objeto do frame 1 (fita de evidência) na mesma posição; a última palavra ("file") repete a primeira ("file").
2. **Pergunta eco:** o frame 1 abre "Who was she?" e o payoff fecha com a mesma pergunta respondida pela metade — o resto só no long.
3. **Número espelho:** "fifty years" do frame 1 volta como última frase ("fifty years, to the day") antes do corte.
4. **Cena espelho:** o corredor/documento do frame 1 reaparece no último frame com a mesma luz e enquadramento (emenda visual).
5. **Loop aberto:** terminar no meio da frase do arquivo ("…and that's when the second letter arrived") — o início mostra a chegada da primeira.

## CTAs (que não quebram o loop)

- **Falado (fim):** "The [STATE] [YEAR] file I left out — tonight, 8pm ET, full episode."
- **Comentário fixado:** pergunta do caso + link do long ("Which detail did the jury miss? Full [STATE] [YEAR] file: [link]").
- **Related Video:** apontar para o long do dia (nunca para o canal em geral).

## Proibido neste nicho

- Gore: corpo, sangue, close de ferimento, descrição de sofrimento — em qualquer camada (fala, texto, thumbnail, áudio).
- Nome de pessoa viva ligada a caso sem "alleged"/"suspect"/"accused".
- Teoria apresentada como fato; número de views ou "this case shocked the internet".
- Data/local antes do gancho; "in this video", "subscribe now", CTA genérico.
- Prometer resolução que o long não entrega; explorar a família; sensacionalismo no frame 1.
