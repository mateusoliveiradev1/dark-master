# Hooks — Small town secrets

> Sistema: `references/31` (Shorts) + `references/03` (Y1–Y11). Use o arquétipo, não a frase.

## Long-form (10 ganchos)

Cada gancho: 1–2 frases, sem data/local antes do gancho, sem abstração.

1. "The first letter knew what the family had done that weekend. It arrived on Monday."
2. "The vault was open, the alarm was armed, and the teller was still sitting at her window."
3. "The 911 call lasted nine minutes. The last four were just him breathing."
4. "The fire took the whole office. One file cabinet came out clean."
5. "Five men left in one car. The car came back empty, parked, with the keys inside."
6. "'Lock your doors,' the letter said. 'I know which room is yours.'"
7. "For 41 years the county kept the file sealed. The town never needed to read it."
8. "They searched one field for three days. The right one was across the same road."
9. "You're the last person who saw her, and the police have already asked you twice."
10. "He left at 9. She called at 9:04. The call log ends there."

## Shorts — frame 1 (15 hooks)

| # | Arquétipo (ref 31) | Texto na tela (≤6) | Falado (≤8) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Impossibilidade completa | LOCKED FROM OUTSIDE | Her keys were inside, under her coat. |
| 2 | Pergunta em 2ª pessoa | WHO WROTE THEM? | Who mailed twenty letters and never got caught? |
| 3 | Contradição verificada | ALARM WAS ARMED | The alarm was armed. He walked past it. |
| 4 | Número + stake | 5 MEN. 1 CAR. 0. | Five men left. The car came back empty. |
| 5 | Relógio/countdown | 9:04 TO 9:13 | Nine minutes on the line, then silence. |
| 6 | Antes/depois impossível | 2 HOURS AFTER | This letter arrived two hours after he did. |
| 7 | Objeto-símbolo | ONE ENVELOPE | All the town kept was one envelope. |
| 8 | Citação/diálogo | LOCK YOUR DOORS | "'Lock your doors. I know your room.'" |
| 9 | POV/imersão | LAST TO SEE HER | You're the last one who saw her. |
| 10 | Segredo/proibido | SEALED 41 YEARS | This county file stayed sealed forty-one years. |
| 11 | Loop declarado | WATCH THE WINDOW | Watch the window. It's still open. |

| 14 | Escolha impossível | SAVE THE FILE | Save the evidence, or save the witness. |
| 15 | Objeto-símbolo (2ª) | KEYS INSIDE | They found her car. Keys inside. Nobody there. |

## Loops (5 emendas)

1. **Estrada vazia:** fecha no mesmo enquadramento do frame 1 (objeto parado, mesma luz); a última palavra repete a primeira ("empty" → "empty") e o beat volta ao início.
2. **Loop aberto:** terminar no meio da frase de abertura — o fim diz "...and the second letter arrived" e o frame 1 começa em "the first letter".
3. **Pergunta→eco:** o último segundo repete o texto na tela do frame 1, na mesma tipografia, sem CTA falado.
4. **Documento→mesa:** o corte final volta para a mesa/mapa do frame 1; o som do papel fechando emenda a frase inicial.
5. **Silêncio→ligação:** o áudio corta no meio do toque de telefone que abre o Short; o loop acontece pelo som, não pela imagem.

## CTAs (que não quebram o loop)

- **Falado (fim):** "The rest of the file — documents, timeline, map — is on the channel tonight." (específico do caso; emenda com o loop)
- **Comentário fixado:** "What would you have done on that road? Full file: [link do long]"
- **Related Video:** apontar para o long do dia na série "Small Town Files".

## Proibido neste nicho

- Gore, corpo ou sangue no hook, no frame 1 ou na thumb.
- Psychic/paranormal como prova — no máximo [REPORTADO], atribuído.
- Nome de pessoa viva sem "alleged"; acusação apresentada como fato.
- Reencenação em 1ª pessoa de vítima falecida (política do YouTube desde jan/2024).
- Casos com foco em violência contra menores e sensitive events (mass shooting, desastre ativo).
- Data + local antes do gancho; meta-linguagem; promessa que o long não entrega.
